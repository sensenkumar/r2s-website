Frequently Asked Questions

How is pricing determined?

Pricing is based on the number of rooms, the modules you select, and your support tier. Every plan is quoted individually so you only pay for what your property needs — reach out for a custom proposal.

Can I add modules as my property grows?

Yes. You can add any of the seven modules at any time without migrating data or disrupting existing operations. Your account manager will help you scope the change and adjust your plan.

Is there a long-term contract?

No long-term contract is required on the Essential or Professional plans. Enterprise customers can choose annual terms for additional savings, but month-to-month options are always available.

What does onboarding include?

Onboarding covers data migration, staff training, hardware setup, and a guided go-live. Higher tiers include faster, more hands-on onboarding, up to full on-site white-glove setup for Enterprise.

Do you support multi-property hotel groups?

Yes. The Enterprise plan includes a multi-property management console that lets you monitor and manage every location from a single dashboard, with consolidated reporting across your portfolio.
